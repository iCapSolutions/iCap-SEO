# iCap SEO

iCap SEO is a WordPress plugin that checks your pages for common SEO problems, explains what it finds in plain
language, and fixes most of it for you, with one click or with a preview you approve first. 35 of its 38 checks
and their fixes run on your own site with no account. An optional cloud service adds site-wide scans, 3 Premium
checks that need an outside view of your site, AI-assisted drafts, and Google Search Console and Analytics reports.

Built and maintained by [iCapSolutions](https://www.icapsolutions.com).

## What it does

- **Full-site and per-page scoring** across on-page SEO, technical crawlability, content quality, structured
  data, image optimization, and internal/external links.
- **One-click automatic fixes** for most issues the moment you approve them — titles, meta descriptions,
  headings, alt text, structured data, canonical URLs, and more.
- **Preview-before-publish** for content changes (expanding thin pages, simplifying hard-to-read paragraphs)
  — nothing is written to your site until you explicitly approve it.
- **AI-assisted drafting** for several fixes: Claude reads each page's actual content to draft meta
  descriptions, titles, headings, expanded content, readability rewrites, and spelling/grammar corrections
  tailored to that page — not filled in from a generic template. Image alt text is generated by a vision
  model looking at the image itself.
- **A Setup Wizard and dashboard** for connection setup, site health scoring, and a full recommendation
  catalog per page.
- **Social sharing previews, a redirect manager with a 404 log, optional search-engine indexing pings, an
  llms.txt file, and Local SEO** — always free, no registration required: Open Graph and X Card tags for link
  previews, a simple old-URL-to-new-URL redirect tool that also tracks broken links visitors hit, IndexNow
  pings to Bing/Yandex on publish/update/delete (off by default), a generated `/llms.txt` for AI assistants,
  and LocalBusiness structured data for businesses with a physical location.

See [`docs/seo-checks-catalog.md`](docs/seo-checks-catalog.md) for the full list of checks and how each one
gets fixed.

## Installation

1. Download the latest release ZIP from [Releases](https://github.com/iCapSolutions/iCap-SEO/releases).
2. In your WordPress admin, go to **Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin**.
3. Upload the ZIP, install, and activate **iCap SEO**.

## Getting started

1. Open **iCap SEO → Content Scores** and pick a page to see the free checks and apply fixes. No account needed.
2. Optional, for the cloud service: in **Setup Wizard**, request a registration token by email, enter it in
   **Settings**, then click **Test Connection** and **Request Credentials & Register Site**.
3. Click **Trigger Full Scan** to run your first site-wide scan, then review **Overview** and **Content Scores**.

See [`docs/getting-started.md`](docs/getting-started.md) for a full walkthrough, including premium
activation and troubleshooting.

## Free vs. Premium

- **Free**: 35 checks that run on your own site (title, meta description, H1, HTTPS, robots meta rules and
  robots.txt, security headers, content depth, readability, heading and paragraph structure, canonical URL,
  structured data, images, internal links, external references, and internal nofollow links) with one-click
  fixes or guidance, plus social sharing previews, redirects with a 404 log, optional IndexNow pings, llms.txt,
  and Local SEO.
- **Premium**: 3 checks run by the iCap SEO scan service from outside your site — page reachability, and
  broken internal and external links.
