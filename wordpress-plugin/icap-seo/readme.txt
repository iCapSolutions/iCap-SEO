== Description ==

iCap SEO runs a real, cloud-connected SEO scan of your site — 31 checks across 6 categories — explains what it finds in plain language, and fixes most of it for you, automatically or with a preview you approve first.

**What it does**

* **Full-site and per-page scoring** across on-page SEO, technical crawlability, content quality, structured data, image optimization, and internal/external links.
* **One-click automatic fixes** for most issues the moment you approve them — titles, meta descriptions, headings, alt text, structured data, canonical URLs, and more.
* **Preview-before-publish** for content changes (expanding thin pages, simplifying hard-to-read paragraphs) — nothing is written to your site until you explicitly approve it.
* **AI-assisted drafting** for several fixes: page content is read to draft meta descriptions, titles, headings, expanded content, and readability rewrites tailored to that page, not filled in from a generic template. Image alt text is generated by a vision model looking at the image itself.
* **A Setup Wizard and dashboard** for connection setup, site health scoring, and a full recommendation catalog per page.
* **Search Console and Analytics integration** — connect Google Search Console and Google Analytics (GA4) to see real indexing status and traffic data alongside your scores.
* **Social sharing previews, a redirect manager with a 404 log, automatic search-engine indexing pings, an llms.txt file, and Local SEO** — always free, independent of the 31-check catalog: Open Graph and X Card tags for link previews, a simple old-URL-to-new-URL redirect tool that also tracks pages visitors hit that don't exist, IndexNow pings to Bing/Yandex on publish, update, or delete, a generated `/llms.txt` for AI assistants, and LocalBusiness structured data (address, phone, hours) for businesses with a physical location.

**Free vs. Premium**

* **Free**: a real baseline on-page audit — title tags, meta descriptions, headings, content depth — plus social sharing previews, redirects with a 404 log, IndexNow pings, llms.txt, and Local SEO. Fully usable on its own, no credit card required.
* **Premium**: the full 31-check catalog — technical crawlability, security headers, content quality and readability, structured data, image optimization, and link health — plus AI-assisted drafting.

iCap SEO is a cloud-connected plugin: scans run against the iCap SEO service, which your site talks to after you register it with a token. See the FAQ below for what that means for your data.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory, or install it directly through the WordPress **Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin** screen.
2. Activate the plugin through the **Plugins** screen in WordPress.
3. Go to **iCap SEO → Settings** and confirm the API Base URL (pre-filled by default).
4. Enter the registration token provided by iCapSolutions.
5. Go to **iCap SEO → Setup Wizard**, click **Test Connection**, then **Request Credentials & Register Site**.
6. Click **Trigger Full Scan** to run your first scan, then review results under **Overview** and **Content Scores**.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Do I need an account to use this plugin? =

Yes. iCap SEO connects your site to the iCap SEO cloud service to run scans and generate fixes. The free tier requires registration but no payment.

= Does the free version actually do anything, or is it just a paywall? =

The free tier runs a real baseline audit — title tags, meta descriptions, headings, and content depth — with real scores and real one-click fixes, not a locked preview.

= Will this plugin change my content without asking? =

No. Structural and content-level fixes (expanded content, reworded paragraphs) are always shown as a preview you approve before anything is written to your site. Simple fixes like alt text and meta descriptions can be applied with one click once you choose to apply them.

= What does "cloud-connected" mean for my data? =

Scans read your public page content to generate scores and suggested fixes; requests are authenticated with a site-specific token issued during registration. No content is published or changed without your explicit action in the dashboard.

= Why isn't a scan starting? =

Confirm registration completed successfully and that credentials are saved under **Settings**. If Premium checks aren't appearing, use **Check Billing Status** to confirm your subscription is active.

= Where can I see the full list of checks? =

The in-plugin dashboard lists every check with its category and current status under **Content Scores**.
